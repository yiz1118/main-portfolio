# Surface brief: portfolio site (home, work, cases, services, about, contact)

Scope: every route of main-portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads), with Persuade duties (Start a Project always one step away).

Audience: founders, brands and small businesses, agencies, weighted equally. Job: judge whether one developer can own their build end to end. Action: Start a Project (contact page, WhatsApp, email). Proof: six real concept builds with live sites, source, and screenshot provenance. Constraints: no fabricated clients, testimonials, portraits, or signatures; concept labels stay explicit.

Memorable moment: the hero credits ledger, Discover / Design / Build / Test / Launch, every line credited to Alson Chua, beside the current work mounted as a numbered plate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Edition of Six. The portfolio is a fine-art print portfolio: six works pulled, signed and numbered by one hand. Ownership is shown as credits, edition numbers and provenance, refusing the category's split hero, tinted project cards and eyebrow-numbered sections.

OWN-WORLD: Rag-white sheet (#f2f1ec), press-black ink (#151413), graphite pencil (#5e5c57), one vermilion chop (#cf3a1e) used only as a mark. Works sit as plates: lighter mounted sheets with a debossed plate mark and a typeset italic inscription (title, n/6, A. Chua 2026). Schibsted Grotesk for display and UI, Libre Caslon italic for inscriptions and catalogue notes. Hairline rules, no cards, no shadows beyond the mount.

STORY: The visitor sees one person credited at every stage, browses six numbered works in very different worlds, reads each as a catalogue entry with provenance, and commissions the next one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 5 of 12 columns, the h1 credits ledger set large with ruled rows and "Alson Chua" credited in italic on each, then one plain sentence and Start a Project as a press-black plate button. Right 7 columns, the selected work as a mounted plate with inscription; six numbered sheet tabs on its edge, keys 1 to 6 strike one forward. Mobile: ledger, then plate, then a six-tab row.

FORM: Fine-art print portfolio (catalogue raisonné, plate marks, chop), position 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 3eea6f70. Raises: all six present and struck forward without cross-fade (cathode); direct 1 to 6 keys (teletext); lines draw rather than fade (secession); live Malaysia studio clock (signal); one process stage per full-width row with its result (forge); labels live on the object, no eyebrows (quote grammar). Signature interaction: plates are pulled on entry (paper wipes off the image while the plate mark draws), and a plate morphs into its case hero via view transitions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
