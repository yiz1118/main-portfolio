# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients evaluating whether to hire Alson Chua for a digital build. Confirmed as equally important: founders and startups (MVPs, SaaS, dashboards, AI products), brands and small businesses (websites and online stores), and agencies or studios looking for a freelance development partner. They arrive from a link, scan the work, judge craft and reliability, and decide whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A freelance portfolio hub for Alson Chua, Independent Web & App Developer, based in Malaysia and working with clients worldwide. It presents six independent concept projects, explains each through a case study, links to the live builds and source, and turns interest into a project conversation by email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or a prepared brief. Success is a qualified visitor starting a project conversation.

## Positioning

End-to-end ownership: one person carries a project from idea through design, build, testing, and launch, and hands it over cleanly. The six concepts prove range across automotive, SaaS, fashion e-commerce, hospitality, architecture, and skincare commerce, each built as its own brand world rather than a reskinned template. Game-development roots inform an interactive, systems-minded approach.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse on desktop and phone, often from a shared link or LinkedIn. Each project has a live deployed site, a public GitHub repository, and six real screenshots captured from the deployed interface with provenance receipts. Contact runs through direct email, a pre-filled WhatsApp message, LinkedIn, or a project-brief form that prepares an email/WhatsApp message or copies the brief. Optional server-side email delivery exists but is unconfigured by default.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4; deployed on Vercel.
- Routes: `/`, `/work`, `/work/[slug]` (six cases), `/services`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/api/contact`; `/projects` redirects to `/work`.
- Project content lives in `data/projects.ts`; identity and contact destinations in `lib/site.ts`.
- Services offered: business websites, web applications, MVP development, AI integrations, maintenance and improvements, e-commerce.
- Process: discover, design, build, test, launch.
- The six projects are Independent Concept Projects (2026), not client work.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and title: Alson Chua, Independent Web & App Developer. Location line: Malaysia, working with clients worldwide.
- Primary call to action: Start a Project.
- The previous visual identity (cream paper, cobalt, Manrope, "a." monogram, "Ideas into real products.") is confirmed replaceable.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six concept projects with live URLs, GitHub repositories, case content, and 36 real WebP screenshots in `public/projects/{slug}/` with receipts in `artifacts/integration/screenshot-receipts.json`.
- No portrait, testimonials, client logos, awards, or measured business outcomes exist. Never fabricate them; never imply the concepts were paid client work.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work before describing it; real screenshots and live links are the proof.
2. Make ownership visible: the path from idea to launch is the story.
3. Be truthful about scope: concept labels, demo limits, and claims stay explicit.
4. Contact is always one step away and works without backend configuration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard operation, visible focus, 44px touch targets, readable contrast, reduced-motion alternatives, content readable without JavaScript.
